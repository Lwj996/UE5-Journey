## ③ Fab 资源商店 · The Fab Marketplace

**🇨🇳**
下载 UE 期间我去逛了 Fab（原 Marketplace + Quixel + Sketchfab 合并后的 Epic 官方资产平台），有两个让我意外的发现：

1. **很多 3D 模型质量很低**。我以为官方平台都是精品，结果良莠不齐，要仔细挑。
2. **我之前收藏过的一些资源已经 404 了**。原来收藏不等于永久保存，下架就是没了。

**反思**：以后遇到喜欢的资源，**收藏 + 立即下载**双保险，不能只靠收藏。

**🇬🇧** *(AI-translated)*
While UE was downloading I spent some time on Fab (Epic's official asset platform, formed by merging the old Marketplace, Quixel, and Sketchfab). Two surprises:

1. **A lot of the 3D models are low quality.** I assumed the official platform would only carry premium assets — turns out it's mixed, and you have to pick carefully.
2. **Some of the assets I had favorited before are now 404.** A favorite is not a backup — once an asset is delisted, it's gone.

**Lesson**: when I find an asset I like, I'll **favorite AND download immediately** as a double safety net.

**🇨🇳**
我特地花时间查了 UE 编辑器界面的一些英文词汇。我相信一句话：

> **想要学会某个技术，得先学会它的专业词汇。否则它只会以一个抽象的概念存在脑海里，不利于记忆，也不利于精确表达。**

英文界面的好处：以后查文档、看 YouTube、读源码全部对得上号。

**🇬🇧** *(AI-translated)*
I deliberately spent time looking up the English terms for elements of the UE editor UI. I believe in this principle:

> **To truly learn a technology, you must first learn its vocabulary. Otherwise it only lives in your mind as an abstract concept — bad for memory, and bad for precise expression.**

The benefit of using the English UI: from now on, documentation, YouTube tutorials, and source code will all line up perfectly.
